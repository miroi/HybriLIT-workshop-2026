# Diels–Alder cycloaddition

Standard reaction enthalpy of the Diels–Alder $[4+2]$ cycloaddition of 1,3-butadiene and ethene to form cyclohexene, evaluated via MOPAC, xTB, and MACE, including comparative benchmark tables and a cyclopentadiene challenge.

### Overview & Benchmark
The repository provides benchmark comparisons against the experimental NIST gas-phase reaction enthalpy of **$-166.7\text{ kJ/mol}$** at $298.15\text{ K}$, detailing electronic energy changes, zero-point energy (ZPE) adjustments, and method-specific errors for MACE-OFF23, MOPAC PM7.
